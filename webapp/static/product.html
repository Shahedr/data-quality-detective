What the software does

  • Find missing and inconsistent cell values, duplicate rows, unusual numbers, and certain invalid dropdown choices.
  • Review multiple Excel worksheets, with warnings about hidden sheets and cached formulas.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool repair my workbook?

No. It produces diagnostic reports; you remain responsible for verifying findings and editing the original workbook.

Does the web app run macros?

No. The hosted demo and planned hosted account workspace never execute VBA. A separate local Windows tool can run an explicitly approved named macro, but this is not a cloud feature.

What about sensitive personal or financial data?

Do not upload sensitive, regulated, or confidential data to the public demo. It does not accept customer uploads, and the private workspace has not been approved for such processing.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

There is currently nothing to buy or cancel. Before paid subscriptions launch, the verified refund/dispute and cancellation policies and customer-support contact information will be displayed here and during checkout.

Is anything shipped?

No. This is software delivered online. No physical shipping is involved.
