BETA DATA PRACTICES • NOT AN APPROVED FINAL LEGAL NOTICE

Privacy and data handling information

Important: This page describes planned data flows, not a legally reviewed privacy policy. The private upload service stays disabled until the operator verifies, completes and approves an enforceable notice with their actual identity, address, contact and vendor agreements. Do not submit regulated or highly confidential data.

Public sample demo

The public preview analyzes only bundled fictional sample information. Customer spreadsheet uploads are blocked. Hosting providers may process ordinary operational request data, including IP addresses, browser and request metadata, for hosting/security purposes.

Proposed private-beta processing

If activated by the operator, account creation and email authentication are provided by Supabase. A customer-selected CSV/XLSX/XLSM file is transmitted over HTTPS to the hosted analysis service, which parses the file and sends findings back to the authenticated user. The application does not intentionally write uploaded workbook contents or filenames into its database, but multipart processing may spool bytes to ephemeral storage and infrastructure providers may create operational logs. Downloaded reports may contain confidential content; the customer is responsible for controlling where exports are shared.

Data types

Identity provider: email address, authentication identifiers, session records and account security events. SaaS database: user ID, monthly/daily analysis counts, subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and processed webhook IDs. Payments: Stripe handles payment details and billing records, which the application does not directly collect.

Purpose and recipients

Use account data to authenticate, meter usage, manage subscriptions, and protect the service. Hosting provider Vercel, account/database provider Supabase, and payments provider Stripe may process data under their own agreements. No ad-targeting or sale of uploaded worksheet data is intended. Actual subprocessors and transfer locations must be confirmed before launch.

Where records live

If private service access is ever enabled, login identity and sessions will be stored in Supabase Auth, and the application's four private PostgreSQL tables will store account IDs, subscription references, per-day and per-month usage counts, and Stripe webhook identifiers. Stripe maintains its own payment and invoice data. The uploaded workbook bytes are processed by the Vercel-hosted application; the application does not deliberately create a saved workbook library. Temporary request storage, network/infrastructure logs, third-party SDK delivery and provider backups require separate review. The Supabase database is configured in US East (Ohio), which does not prove that all other provider processing stays in Ohio.

Browser sessions and cookies

When login is eventually enabled, Supabase may store authentication tokens in the browser to maintain login sessions. Providers may use required security and delivery mechanisms. The full cookie and logging inventory has not been verified; no claim of zero tracking, cookies, or logging is made.

Administration

A proposed private owner console, if separately enabled, uses signed authentication, an explicitly approved account ID and multi-factor authentication to view aggregate service counts. It is not designed to expose spreadsheet contents or customer email lists.

Retention, deletion, security

Uploaded workbook content is not intentionally saved as a lasting application record; any temporary spooled data is subject to the hosting runtime lifecycle. Usage counters remain until account deletion or administrative retention cleanup. Stripe may retain billing records according to legal requirements and Stripe's policies; Supabase identity deletion requires support processing. The service uses signed authentication tokens and account-based authorization; no representation of formal certification or absolute security is made.

Rights and contact

For access, correction, deletion, or privacy requests, use the operator privacy address once it is displayed in the private workspace. If no contact address is visible, private beta is not ready to accept customers. Applicable rights vary by jurisdiction.

Operator must review applicable notice-at-collection, cookie/analytics, international transfers, breach notices, age restrictions, and retention requirements before launch. This informational draft is not legal advice.
