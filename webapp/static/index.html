    Run locally — keep your files on your computer

    Process up to 1 GB locally

    Analyze larger files on your Windows PC or Mac without uploading them to this website. The local app is free and does not require an account or Microsoft Excel.

    Local analysis runs on your computer. Temporary processing files and downloaded reports are stored on your device. Read the privacy notice.

    1. Download the app for your computer

    No Python installation or commands are needed. Download the ZIP file, then follow the steps for your computer below. Allow at least 5 GB of free disk space when cleaning large files.

    Download for Windows Download for Mac — Apple Silicon Download for Mac — Intel

    Community preview for 64-bit Windows and Mac. These downloads are not publisher-verified; your computer may display a security warning. Read the guidance below before opening them.

    2. Extract and open the app

    Windows — download and open
    1. Select Download for Windows above. Wait for the download to finish.
    2. Open File Explorer (the folder icon on your taskbar), then select Downloads.
    3. Find data-quality-detective-windows-x64.zip. Right-click it, select Extract All, then select Extract. Do not open the app from inside the ZIP.
    4. Open the extracted DataQualityDetective folder. Double-click DataQualityDetective.exe (it may appear without “.exe”). Keep all the extracted files together.
    5. A small app window opens, followed by the workspace in your browser. Keep that app window open while you work. Next time, open the same application again; you do not need to download it again.
    Mac — download and open
    1. Open the Apple menu > About This Mac. If you see Chip with an M-series name, choose the Apple Silicon download. If you see an Intel processor, choose Intel.
    2. Select the matching download above. Open Finder > Downloads after it finishes.
    3. Double-click the downloaded ZIP to extract it, if your browser has not already extracted it.
    4. Drag DataQualityDetective.app into Applications. Open Applications and double-click the app.
    5. A small app window opens, followed by the workspace in your browser. Keep the app window open while you work. Next time, open it from Applications.
    If your computer displays a security warning

    These free community downloads do not have a verified publisher certificate or Apple notarization. We have not purchased those services. A warning about an unknown publisher does not establish whether a file is safe.

    Download only from our official release page. That page includes SHA-256 checksums to check whether a download has changed. A matching checksum is not a security certification.

    On Windows, an unknown-publisher warning may offer More info and Run anyway. Proceed only if you have verified the source and choose to trust the download. On Mac, follow Apple’s instructions for opening an app from an unidentified developer. Your computer or workplace may prohibit this.

    If a warning identifies malware, stop and contact support. Do not disable antivirus protection or your computer’s security settings.

    The app stays free. To discuss supporting future publisher verification and maintenance, contact Shahed. No contribution is required.

    3. Open and review your file

    1. The app opens in your browser. Look for FREE LOCAL WORKSPACE at the top. Keep the small app window open while you work.
    2. Choose or drop your file in the upload area at the top. Files over 10 MB open the large-file tools automatically. You can also expand Large files · up to 1 GB · on this computer. Choose your CSV, XLSX or XLSM file, then select Inspect large file.
    3. Review the overview and choose a worksheet if needed. To clean the file, open Prepare a cleaned large-file copy, preview the changes and download a separate copy.
    4. Finish or cancel any processing, then select Quit in the small app window. Closing the browser tab alone does not stop the app.
    Need help?

    The browser does not open: select Open workspace in the small app window. You can also type the local address displayed there into your browser.

    The app does not start: check that you extracted the ZIP, chose the correct download for your computer, and kept the application’s files together. Read any startup message in the app window. If it still fails, contact support with your operating system and the exact error message; do not include private spreadsheet data.

    A file is rejected: read the message shown in the app. File type, workbook structure and available disk space also affect what can be processed. Password-protected files are not supported.

    File limits and privacy

    The local limit is 1,073,741,824 bytes per file (1 GiB, approximately 1.07 GB). We use “1 GB” as the rounded label. One large file is processed at a time. Large-file mode provides an overview and selected cleaning tools; charts, templates and the detailed grid are available in the smaller-file workspace.

    Your original file stays unchanged. Cleaning creates a separate copy. Formulas and macros are preserved in supported Excel copies, but changes to input cells can affect their results. Only enable macros from a source you trust.

    Use a folder outside cloud-sync services when working with private data. Keep the local app accessible only on your own computer. Downloaded reports remain on your device until you delete them.
